# 1. Project Overview

## 1.1 Product identity

- **Product name:** Mahi-Tech Platform
- **Product type:** Agency Business Management System
- **Business owner:** Mahi-Tech Consultant & Services
- **Tagline:** Your Vision. Our Technology.
- **Brand colors:** Blue `#1457D9`, Midnight Black `#111827`, White `#FFFFFF`
- **Initial audience:** Agency owner and authorized team members
- **Additional audience:** Clients through a controlled client portal

## 1.2 Vision

Provide one secure workspace for managing the agency's customer lifecycle and operations: lead capture, client onboarding, project delivery, proposals, invoices, payment tracking, expenses, documents, communication and reporting.

## 1.3 Business problem

Agency information can become fragmented across spreadsheets, chats, documents and separate tools. The platform aims to establish a central source of truth for customers, work, documents and financial activity.

## 1.4 Product goals

1. Centralize business records.
2. Make lead follow-up visible and consistent.
3. Track project scope, deadlines, milestones and tasks.
4. Produce consistent proposals, quotations and invoices.
5. Reconcile invoices, payments and expenses.
6. Provide clients controlled visibility into their own work.
7. Improve operational reporting and accountability.
8. Establish a secure, maintainable foundation for future agency growth.

## 1.5 Scope

### Included
- Authentication, staff accounts, roles and permissions
- Dashboard and activity feed
- CRM, leads, pipeline and follow-ups
- Client organizations and contacts
- Projects, milestones, tasks and updates
- Proposals and quotations
- Invoices, payment records and receipts
- Expenses and financial summaries
- Private documents and generated PDFs
- Client portal and feedback
- Team assignment and workload visibility
- Notifications and reminders
- Reports and exports
- Audit history, security, testing and cloud operations

### Excluded
- AI Development Studio, AI code generation, AI architecture generation, code editor, AI website builder and generated-code execution. Separate team ownership.
- Public SaaS self-service signup and commercial multi-tenancy in the first release.
- Native Android/iOS apps.
- Payroll and full HR management.
- Inventory and warehouse management.
- Payment card data storage or payment gateway processing unless separately approved.
- Statutory accounting replacement.

## 1.6 Success measures

Initial measures to baseline during pilot:
- Percentage of new leads recorded in the platform.
- Lead follow-up completion rate.
- Percentage of active projects with owner, due date and status.
- Time required to prepare and send a proposal/invoice.
- Accuracy of outstanding balance calculations.
- Number of client access-control defects.
- Availability and backup restore success.
- User adoption by owner and team.

Targets should be agreed after collecting baseline operating data; do not invent targets before a pilot.

## 1.7 Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Lead | A potential customer not yet converted to a client |
| Client | A person or organization receiving agency services |
| Client contact | A person associated with a client organization |
| Project | A defined body of work for a client |
| Milestone | A significant project checkpoint |
| Task | An assignable unit of work |
| Proposal | A document describing proposed work and commercial terms |
| Quotation | A priced offer for requested services |
| Invoice | A request for payment for delivered or agreed services |
| Payment | A recorded receipt of money |
| Payment allocation | The portion of a payment applied to an invoice |
| Client portal | Restricted interface for client users |
| Audit event | Record of a security- or business-relevant action |
