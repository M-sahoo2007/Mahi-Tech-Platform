# Open Decisions and Project Templates

## Decisions required before implementation

1. Confirm backend choice: Django + DRF.
2. Confirm Vite and Tailwind CSS for React setup.
3. Confirm authentication approach and browser token/session storage.
4. Confirm currency and tax/GST rules.
5. Confirm invoice numbering and financial-year policy.
6. Confirm client portal account model.
7. Confirm cloud provider, region, domain and budget.
8. Confirm email provider and sender domain.
9. Confirm refund/credit-note scope.
10. Confirm privacy, retention and deletion policy.
